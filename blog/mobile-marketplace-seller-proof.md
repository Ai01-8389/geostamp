# I Sell on Facebook Marketplace — GeoStamp on My Phone Saves Me From 'Is This Still Available?'

I sell a lot of stuff on Facebook Marketplace. Furniture, electronics, baby gear my kids outgrew — if it's not nailed down, I've probably listed it.

The biggest headache? Buyers who agree to pick up and then claim they "couldn't find the address" or worse, claim the item was damaged when they got there.

## The Usual Problem

When you list an item, you take photos with your phone. Those photos have GPS data in the EXIF. By the time you've uploaded them to Facebook, that data is gone.

Then when a buyer messages you "is this still available?" and you say "yes, come pick it up at 123 Oak Street," they show up at the wrong house, take a photo of your item on their phone (losing the location again), and you have no proof of where the exchange happened.

## How I Use GeoStamp Before Every Pickup

Now my routine looks like this:

### When I list an item
1. Take photos of the item with my phone on my front porch
2. Open `geostamp.top` on my phone
3. Stamp the photos with my address and date
4. Use the stamped photos in my listing

Result: the buyer sees my address on the photo itself. "Pick up at 123 Oak Street" is not just text — it's on the image.

### At the pickup
When the buyer arrives, I take one final photo of the item with them (or their car) and stamp it with GeoStamp right there. Now I have proof:
- The item existed at my address
- The date and time of pickup
- The condition at handoff

### No more disputes
I had a buyer once claim I gave them a broken table. They sent me a photo of a cracked table at their house — could have been any table, anywhere.

I sent back my GeoStamp-stamped photo showing the table in perfect condition at my address, dated and timed at the pickup moment. They stopped responding.

## Why It Works

- Your phone camera captures GPS automatically
- GeoStamp makes that data visible on the image before you upload anywhere
- Facebook/WhatsApp/email strips EXIF — but they can't strip what's burned into the image
- A visible address on the photo is better than any "I swear I told you" text message

## The 60-Second Workflow

All from my phone, all in the browser:

```
Take photos with phone → open geostamp.top → upload → download stamped versions → list on Marketplace
```

No app install. No account. Just a browser bookmark on my home screen.

Since I started doing this, every pickup has been smoother. Buyers see the address on the photo before they even message me. And I have proof of every handoff.
