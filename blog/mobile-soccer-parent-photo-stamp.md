# Every Weekend Soccer Game Blurs Together — Until I Started Stamping Photos on My Phone

My son plays U10 soccer. We go to tournaments almost every weekend in spring. The fields all look the same: green grass, white lines, a goal at each end.

After a season of taking hundreds of photos on my phone and months later not knowing which game was which, I found a simple fix.

## The Problem: Too Many Games, Too Little Memory

Last season I had 340 photos across 12 games. By the end of the season, I couldn't tell you:
- Which game was the one he scored his first goal?
- Was that muddy field photo from the tournament in Mill Creek or the one in Lynnwood?
- Did I take that photo on Saturday or Sunday?

The photos were all named `IMG_7XXX.jpeg`. The location data was buried in EXIF. I never shared them in a way that preserved it.

## The GeoStamp Routine: 90 Seconds Per Game

This season I started a habit. After each game, while sitting in the car waiting for my son to change out of his cleats:

1. Open Chrome on my phone
2. Go to `geostamp.top`
3. Upload the 20-30 best photos from that game
4. Download the stamped versions back to my camera roll

Each photo now has the sports field name, the city, the GPS coordinates, and the date burned right onto the image.

## Three Months Later

I have 6 tournament folders with 180 photos. Every single one shows:
- **Which field** (GPS coordinates + address on the image)
- **Which date** (visible timestamp)
- **Which team's sideline** (the stamp tells me home vs away based on the field location)

Last weekend my son asked "remember that goal I scored from far away?" I opened the photo folder, saw the stamped location and date — "That was the Mill Creek tournament, June 13, field 4, the one where Grandpa came to watch."

## Why It Works for Soccer Parents

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Was this the first goal or the second goal?" | Date on the photo tells you which game |
| "Which field was this at?" | Address stamped on the image |
| "Was this game home or away?" | GPS coordinates tell you the venue |
| "Was his form better in May or April?" | Stamped dates let you compare month by month |

And the best part: I can text the stamped photos to grandparents and they instantly know when and where the game was — no explanation needed.

## The Phone-First Workflow

Everything happens on my phone:
- **Take photos** with the phone camera (GPS recorded automatically)
- **Stamp** with GeoStamp in the browser (before any photo leaves the phone)
- **Share** the stamped versions with family (location travels with the image)

No computer needed. No app install. Just a browser bookmark that takes 90 seconds per game.

This season's photos won't turn into "some soccer game, somewhere." Every one of them has a story attached, because the time and place are right there on the image.
