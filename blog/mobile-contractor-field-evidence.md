# I Use GeoStamp on My Phone to Prove When Work Got Done — My Clients Love It

I'm a general contractor. My days are split between three job sites, two phone calls, and about forty text messages from homeowners asking "did they finish the basement today?"

Before GeoStamp, I'd take photos on my phone and email them to clients. They'd look at the image and ask:
- "Is that the upstairs bathroom or the rental?"
- "When did you take that?"
- "Is that the new work or the old work?"

I'd scroll back through my camera roll trying to remember.

## The Workflow That Changed Everything

I take about 50-80 photos per day across different job sites. Every single one is shot on my phone — it's always in my pocket, always ready.

### How I Use GeoStamp After Every Site Visit

**1. Walk through, snap photos with my phone camera**

The phone automatically embeds GPS coordinates and timestamp into every photo's EXIF data. I don't do anything extra at this stage.

**2. Before leaving the site, open geostamp.top on my phone**

Takes 30 seconds. I upload the batch of photos I just took. Because they haven't been shared or compressed yet, the EXIF data is fully intact.

**3. Annotate and download**

GeoStamp reads the coordinates and burns them onto each image. I get back photos with visible stamps showing:
- GPS coordinates (lat/lon)
- Street address
- Date and time
- Custom prefix I set (e.g., "Oakwood_Reno")

**4. Send to the client right from my phone**

Now when I text "here's the framing" with a photo, they see the location stamp, the date, and the time — all as part of the image. No questions asked.

## The Insurance Claim That Almost Went Wrong

Earlier this year, a pipe burst in a rental unit I manage. I took 40+ photos on my phone documenting the damage, the repair work, and the restoration.

The insurance adjuster asked for "timeline evidence." Before GeoStamp, I would have had to:
- Cross-reference my calendar
- Check photo timestamps in a file manager
- Write a separate document explaining what each photo showed

With GeoStamp, every photo already had the date and location burned into it. I just uploaded the folder and forwarded the link. The claim was approved in 4 days.

## Why Mobile + Browser Works

This is the part contractors appreciate: I don't install anything.

- Open Chrome/Safari on my phone
- Go to `geostamp.top`
- Upload photos from my camera roll
- Download the stamped versions

The whole loop takes under 2 minutes per site. No app stores, no logins, no setup.

## What It Looks Like in Practice

These stamped photos now live in my job folders:

```
Smith_Driveway_2026-06-15_0803.jpeg  →  before pour
Smith_Driveway_2026-06-15_1422.jpeg  →  after pour (same site, different time)
Smith_Driveway_2026-06-16_0901.jpeg  →  cured (next day, same location)
```

A client, an insurance adjuster, or even my future self can open any of these and know exactly: where, when, what.

## The Numbers

Since I started using GeoStamp on mobile 3 months ago:

- **Follow-up questions about location dropped by ~80%**
- **Invoicing disputes: zero** (clients can see the work was done on-site)
- **Time spent organizing photos: cut in half** — the stamp does the organizing for me

A phone in your pocket takes the photo. GeoStamp makes it evidence.
